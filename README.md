# YuLab-S25
Welcome! I am Andrew, an undergraduate researcher at the Haiyuan Lab at Cornell University CALS, Department of Computational Biology. This repository is a codespace for me to compile my previous and ongoing research work, including but not limited to code (Python, R, Shell Script), bioinformatics workflows, data files, figures, and research reports. 

My work in the Yu Lab thus far has spanned **three (3) projects**:

- PPI Prediction Software Evaluation and Benchmarking (with Juheon Chu, Fall 2024 - Spring 2025)
- Structural Homology-Informed Protein-Protein Interaction (PPI) Binary Prediction Modeling (with Juheon Chu and James Hong, Spring 2025 - Present)
- PIONEER3 (with Juheon Chu, Spring 2025 - Present; minor involvement, mostly auxiliary analyses or testing)
